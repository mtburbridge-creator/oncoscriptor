1. Review of immune-related adverse events
   Postow MA, Sidlow R, Hellmann MD. Immune-Related Adverse Events Associated with
   Immune Checkpoint Blockade. N Engl J Med. 2018;378:158-168.
   PMID 29320654. DOI 10.1056/NEJMra1703481.
   Why it matters: the standard narrative review of what immune-related adverse
   events are, which organs they affect, and the general principle of grading and
   immunosuppression. Abstract not available on the record.

2. ASCO management guideline (2021 update)
   Schneider BJ, Naidoo J, Santomasso BD, et al. Management of Immune-Related Adverse
   Events in Patients Treated With Immune Checkpoint Inhibitor Therapy: ASCO Guideline
   Update. J Clin Oncol. 2021;39(36):4073-4126.
   PMID 34724392. DOI 10.1200/JCO.21.01440.
   Why it matters: the US clinical guideline oncologists follow for grading and
   treating these side effects; the script can say that written guidelines exist and
   that management is by grade.

3. ESMO management guideline (2022)
   Haanen J, Obeid M, Spain L, et al. Management of toxicities from immunotherapy:
   ESMO Clinical Practice Guideline for diagnosis, treatment and follow-up.
   Ann Oncol. 2022;33(12):1217-1238.
   PMID 36270461. DOI 10.1016/j.annonc.2022.10.001.
   Why it matters: the European counterpart to the ASCO guideline.

4. Fatal toxicities, systematic review and meta-analysis
   Wang DY, Salem JE, Cohen JV, et al. Fatal Toxic Effects Associated With Immune
   Checkpoint Inhibitors: A Systematic Review and Meta-analysis. JAMA Oncol.
   2018;4(12):1721-1728.
   PMID 30242316. DOI 10.1001/jamaoncol.2018.3923.
   Findings from the abstract (as returned by Europe PMC): combined a WHO
   pharmacovigilance database, records from seven academic centers, and a meta-analysis
   of 112 trials with 19,217 patients. Toxicity-related fatality rates in the trial
   meta-analysis ranged from 0.36 percent to 1.23 percent depending on the regimen.
   Fatal events with anti-CTLA-4 were most often colitis; with anti-PD-1/PD-L1 most
   often pneumonitis, hepatitis, and neurologic effects. Deaths tended to occur early
   after treatment began. Myocarditis had the highest fatality rate among reported cases.
