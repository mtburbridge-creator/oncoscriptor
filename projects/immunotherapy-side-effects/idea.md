title: What to expect from immunotherapy side effects
source: human
found: 2026-10-09
status: new

Checkpoint inhibitors are now standard for many cancers, and people starting them are told the side effects are "different from chemo" without being told what that means. The questions patients seem to ask are practical: which side effects are common and which are rare, how soon they show up and how long they last, what counts as a symptom worth a phone call, whether a side effect means the treatment is working, and what happens to the cancer treatment if a side effect forces a pause. A ten-minute explainer for someone about to start a checkpoint inhibitor, or in their first few cycles, that names the common immune-related side effects in plain language and gives them a clear sense of when to call their team.
